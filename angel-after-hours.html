We invite you to join us at Angel After Hours in Duluth, an upcoming Angel Foundation™ fundraiser to benefit adult cancer patients across northern Minnesota. The event takes place on Wednesday, October 28, 2026 at Greysolon by Black Woods in Duluth. Appetizers and beverages are complimentary, and it is a terrific opportunity for people to learn more about Angel Foundation’s™ expanding presence in northern Minnesota and the hundreds of patients we have served here. The heartwarming program will feature a local patient who has benefited from our resources and leaders making an impact in our cancer care community.

Please RSVP by October 19 via email to danderson@mnangel.org.
