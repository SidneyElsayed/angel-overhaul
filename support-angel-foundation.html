Vic worked in the health and wellness industry for years and always placed a high value on her own health, so she knew something was wrong long before she felt a lump and was finally diagnosed with breast cancer at just 44 years old. Two days after receiving her diagnosis, she was laid off from her job.

Upon being thrust into treatment (as she and her husband refer to it, onto "Planet Cancer"), they faced a number of compounding issues: the loss of Vic''s income, their car needing repairs, ongoing issues with health insurance, and of course, trying to keep up with all of her medical appointments. Vic shared that she does not know how they got through her initial treatment, but she knows her Emergency Financial Assistance grant from Angel Foundation™ was part of the reason. The grant helped with repairs for her car, gas, and freed up money to be used on Ubers to get to and from treatment when other transportation was not available. She shared that she does not know how she would have gotten to her appointments without it.

Receiving her grant from Angel Foundation™ reminded Vic of the people in the world that make the decision to care, even though they don’t have to, and she is incredibly grateful to welcome everyone in the Angel Foundation™ community into her circle of support.

Today, Vic is doing well. She has one more surgery in late 2026 and should finish her final round of chemotherapy in January. She is dipping her toes back into work and is starting to consider the future, possibly going back to school while also keeping up with her newfound passion of making sure women know about the option for a "goldilocks" mastectomy, which is a newer option available for some women that she is especially grateful that her medical team told her about.

When you make a gift to Angel Foundation™, you help local cancer patients like Vic focus on their treatment, rather than on how they are going to get to treatment. Thank you for being part of the circle of support for families like Vic’s!
